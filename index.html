      How ratings work

      England, Wales and Northern Ireland

      5 4 3 2 1 0

      Ratings run from 5 (very good) to 0 (urgent improvement necessary). The detail page also shows the three areas inspected: food handling, the building and how food safety is managed.

      Scotland

      Pass Improve

      Scotland uses its own scheme. Pass means the business met the legal requirements for food hygiene when inspected; Improvement required means it did not. It isn't on the 0 to 5 scale, so it has its own colours.

      Everywhere

      A rating shows how a business was doing on the day it was inspected: hygiene, the state of the premises and how food safety is managed. It is not a guide to how good the food is. Ratings change after each inspection, so check the date. Awaiting means not yet inspected; Exempt means the business doesn't need a rating.

      Tips
      • Switch between List and Map above the results.
      • Moved the map somewhere else? Tap Search this area to load the places you're looking at.
      • Zoom in to see places that share a spot one by one. A pin joined to a small dot by a thin line has been moved slightly so pins don't overlap; the dot marks where it is.
      • Some places are only located by their postcode, so their pin can be a street or so out.
      • Your phone's location can be 10–50 m out in busy streets. If the place you're standing at isn't first in the list, look a few rows down or search by name.
      • Add the app to your home screen: on iPhone, tap Share then Add to Home Screen; on Android, open the browser menu (⋮) then Add to Home screen.
